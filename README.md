# ISO: Basketball

**Retired design dossier, published for anyone who wants to pick it up.**

A portrait mobile basketball concept about creating space through swipe-controlled dribbling, defending the isolation matchup, and finishing the possession with a jumper, dunk, or airborne layup adjustment.

This is research and design documentation only. There is no playable build, engine project, code, purchased asset, or deployed service. The original team retired the project in October 2026 after an internal audit graded it below the team's green-light bar (see [the audit](audits/2026-10-14-ISO-AUDIT.md)). The ideas are here for someone better placed to build them.

## Inventory

- 215 basketball research entries, including 3 explicitly excluded actions.
- 51 animation coverage records; these are requirements, not completed assets.
- 20 fictional high schools and 20 fictional colleges, with names not cleared. The professional league is a single name-pending slot.

All timing values, costs, performance targets, and implementation proposals remain unvalidated unless explicitly stated otherwise. A first prototype must prove control feel, motion quality, and device performance.

## Visual references are not included

The design was developed against a private set of third-party references: a gym training reel used for the main camera, a dunk photograph used for the finishing camera, and two mobile-game character screenshots used as a quality floor. They belong to other people and are not published here. The documents describe what was taken from them in words.
