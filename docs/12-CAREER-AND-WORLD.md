# Career, fictional schools, and practice hubs

## Owner direction

The player progresses through fictional high school, fictional college, and a fictional professional league. Plan twenty high schools and twenty colleges across different US states. The proposed league name is a placeholder pending clearance. Casual clothing belongs especially in private practice and non-team play.

The attached school CSV supplies forty working identities with state, fictional locality, mascot, colors, and modular venue direction. These are authored concepts, not claims that the names are unused. Names, logos, uniforms, and affiliations all need review before public use.

## Keep the career about basketball possessions

Proposal: choose a starting school, build a reputation through short challenge sequences, receive a small set of college choices, then earn a professional opportunity. The twenty schools at each educational tier populate opponents and choices; the player does not need to grind through all forty sequentially.

Represent games as selected isolation possessions or short runs with clear stakes. This preserves the user's central loop without quietly promising a full five-on-five season simulation. Show the larger team's result through a simple match summary if that model is adopted. Exact season length, professional team count, and story content remain open.

Advance difficulty through more disciplined defenders, better recovery, varied spacing, and stronger teammates. Avoid secretly weakening purchased upgrades when moving tiers. Carry earned character progress forward with transparent caps and challenge expectations.

## One practice hub, three career identities

| Stage | Owner-directed setting | Simple purpose |
|---|---|---|
| High school | A home/driveway/backyard court at someone's house | Learn moves, change clothes, practice with friends |
| College | College gym or an outdoor college court | Refine timing, train teammates, practice difficult possessions |
| Professional | Mansion court, reflecting the player's success | Same familiar training loop in a more aspirational setting |

Each hub offers **Play / Train / Style**. Teammate selection sits inside Train. Avoid separate travel, attendance, scheduling, household management, or multiple overlapping progression currencies. Changing career stage changes scenery and opportunities, not the basic menu structure.

For teammate training, select a teammate, select one of the same seven stat categories, preview the cost/effect, and upgrade. The teammate appears automatically in the background session where useful. The user explicitly prioritized simplicity and did not commit to an attendance simulation; do not introduce one.

Practice drills can award a small, clearly capped objective reward without encouraging endless meaningless tapping. Free practice remains available after rewards are exhausted. No energy purchase is required to use the court.

## Environment kit plan

High school identities share a few compact gym layouts. College identities share gym and outdoor-campus layouts. Professional play uses a larger arena kit. Home practice and mansion practice share basketball interaction dimensions while differing in surroundings, finish materials, and atmosphere.

Create individuality through original banners, paint, wall materials, court colors, lighting, background silhouettes, sound ambience, and prop arrangement. Keep the basket, markings, collision boundaries, and camera safety zones consistent enough for controls to remain readable. The hybrid background approach is specified in document 14.

Forty school identities do not imply forty fully modeled venues at initial release. Define identity content separately from venue geometry and release readiness.

## Team and uniform rules

Team games use the fictional school's or league team's uniform. Private practice and designated non-team games use saved casual outfits. Shoes, hair, and approved accessories may carry across both where the mode permits; team presentation restrictions should be explained before entering the court.

Do not create a school identity by recoloring a recognizable real institution's mascot or uniform. Use original marks and typography. Real states provide geographic flavor without claiming affiliation with real schools.
