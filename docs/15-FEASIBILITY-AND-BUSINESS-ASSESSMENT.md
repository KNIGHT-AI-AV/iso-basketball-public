# Feasibility and business assessment

September 26, 2026. Research judgment, not an investment recommendation, revenue forecast, development quote, or guarantee about future AI capabilities.

## Current judgment

This is a credible product concept worth developing on paper and testing in small stages later. The distinctive promise is expressive portrait dribbling that visibly creates a scoring opportunity. The combination of touch control, short possessions, personal style, and shareable finishes can support a clear identity.

It is not yet a validated business. There is no measured demand for this exact control system, no retention cohort, no proven acquisition channel, no demonstrated wardrobe conversion, and no playable evidence of fluid animation. A large catalogue does not close those gaps.

The expensive failure path is commissioning hundreds of animations, outfits, and venues before establishing that strangers enjoy repeating a possession. The disciplined opportunity is to make one small basketball interaction unusually good, then expand the content that players actually value. The owner has not authorized development; the present step is research and specification.

This was a public-document and listing review, not a hands-on comparative playtest. Do not rank the games' actual input latency or animation smoothness from marketing language. The next research pass should use a repeatable observation checklist on devices: time to control, move cancellation, visible contacts, fingers covering action, camera behavior, purchase friction, and replay sharing.

## Graphics and fluidity: separate questions

Good-looking 3D people on a phone are technically credible. A bounded portrait view, a small active cast, restrained camera freedom, modular environments, and a carefully authored main character make an ambitious visual target more plausible.

Fluid basketball depends on hand/ball coordination, support feet, momentum, transition windows, contact reactions, and low input delay. Increasing texture resolution or frame rate does not repair a sliding foot or disconnected gather. The player will notice these flaws repeatedly because basketball places the ball and hands at the center of attention.

Our recommended target is a premium mobile presentation with convincing motion and lighting. Console-scale animation breadth, unrestricted customization, forty unique arenas, and guaranteed peak frame rate on every iPhone should not be bundled into one untested promise. ## AI contribution in 2026 and planning for 2027

| Work | Useful AI-assisted contribution | Required verification |
|---|---|---|
| Research and design | Organize requirements, explore variations, draft specs and test cases | Source checking, owner intent, design consistency |
| Later engineering | Draft tooling, gameplay logic, import scripts, diagnostics, and tests | Build/run evidence, code review, real devices, security and purchase correctness |
| Concept art | Explore original courts, clothing, shoe shapes, palettes, UI composition | Consistent art direction, authorized inputs, original branding |
| 3D generation | Candidate props, base meshes, materials, remeshing and rigging assistance | Topology, UVs, skin deformation, scale, performance, license terms |
| Motion capture | Estimate body motion from appropriately recorded footage | Occlusion cleanup, foot plants, ball/hand events, contacts and retargeting |
| Content operations | Draft item descriptions, organize asset variants, prepare localization | Human review, accuracy, tone, rights and device presentation |

Current vendor tools advertise generation, retopology, auto-rigging, and export workflows. Vendor support guidance also identifies production cleanup needs, and capture guidance identifies occlusion constraints. These are reasons to evaluate them, not proof that a basketball animation library will arrive finished from a prompt.

A sensible architecture can benefit from improved tools in 2027, but the budget cannot depend on an unannounced breakthrough. Keep assets portable and original, maintain source files, and validate each tool against one representative deliverable before scaling. No fixed AI productivity multiplier is justified from this research.

## Commercial proof to seek

First: can a person understand the appeal from a short honest concept sequence? Second: do they want another possession after playing, without being prompted or bribed? Third: do they return voluntarily and value their character? Fourth: will some buy cosmetics while non-paying players still enjoy the game? Finally: can distribution bring players at a cost supported by retained net revenue?

For now, interview likely players and review concept boards without pretending they are a finished game. Separate enthusiastic compliments from willingness to try, return, recommend, or pay. Later measure retention by acquisition cohort and net contribution after fees, refunds, variable operating costs, and acquisition. Avoid importing a generic retention target without matching genre, audience, region, and traffic source.

## Recommendation

Continue research, resolve the portrait control storyboard, define the main character/animation quality brief, and build a defensible scoped estimate before commissioning broad production. The idea deserves a fair test. It does not yet deserve a prediction that it will make millions, and it is not automatically a waste of money. Its value depends on execution and audience response that documents alone cannot establish.
