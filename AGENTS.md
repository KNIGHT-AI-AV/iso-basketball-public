# Project working instructions

## Status

This repository is a retired, public research and design dossier for ISO: Basketball. It contains documentation and data only. It is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0; commercial use needs a separate written agreement (see the [README](README.md)).

## Preserve the design record

Read `docs/README.md` and `docs/18-DECISIONS-AND-DEPENDENCIES.md` before changes. Carry new requirements into affected controls, stats, animation, visual, economy, performance, and future multiplayer documents. Distinguish owner decisions from supporting proposals and unverified targets.

The approved application name is **ISO: Basketball** and the short form is **ISO**. The creative decision does not establish legal clearance.

Do not name the restricted basketball reference product in the design documents, copy its proprietary tables or assets, or add external research citations to the design documents. Use original descriptions and common basketball terminology. Audit records under `audits/` may cite public sources.

Euro steps and post offense are excluded. Preserve explicitly excluded research rows as exclusions, not playable features.

## Media and sensitive material

The third-party visual references used during research are not part of this repository and must not be added. Never commit credentials, personal data, personal machine paths, browser state, or third-party recordings and screenshots. Future production assets must be original or properly licensed.

## Verification

For document changes, check internal links, catalogue IDs/references, counts, exclusions, and consistency with current decisions. Do not claim that documentation proves a tested build, animation quality, legal clearance, revenue, or device performance.
