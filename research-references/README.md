# Visual references (not included)

During research the design was checked against four third-party references:

| Reference | Used for |
|---|---|
| A portrait gym training reel, about 16 seconds at 1080 × 1920 and 60 fps | Main camera framing, full-body visibility, court depth, movement and fabric |
| A rear-view photograph of an airborne dunk | Candidate finishing-camera composition |
| Two mobile-game character screenshots | A minimum quality floor to surpass for faces, materials and fabric |

These belong to other people and were only ever stored privately for research. They are not published in this repository, and nothing here licenses them. The design documents describe what was taken from each reference in words; see [cameras and locations](../docs/02-CAMERAS-AND-LOCATIONS.md) and [character quality and fabric](../docs/19-CHARACTER-QUALITY-AND-FABRIC.md).

Anyone continuing the project should gather or commission their own references and production material with the rights they need.
